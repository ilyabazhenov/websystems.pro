OWA WidgetmacOS 13+ · MCP · free · open source

Meeting in 12 minutes. The link is already here.

A calendar in the menu bar: meetings from Exchange, Google and iCloud on one timeline. The app finds the Teams, Zoom, Webex, Google Meet or KTalk link on its own, and a Join button appears half an hour before the start. Over MCP, your AI assistant sees the calendar too: it finds colleagues and free time, and creates a meeting only after you confirm it in the app.

VoiceGrepmacOS 14.4+ · v1.9 · MCP · 100% local

Call transcripts, right on your Mac.

Records two tracks: your microphone and the call audio. After the call, it transcribes the recording on-device and labels who said what. It recognizes people by voice — you only need to name each one once. Nothing is sent to the cloud. Turn on MCP, and Claude Code, Codex and Cursor can search your meeting archive.

ImpAgentmacOS & Windows · MCP · your own model

Jira, Jenkins and Bitbucket in one window with an agent.

A chat with the model, files, shell and git, with Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, Jenkins, TestOps and Kubernetes right alongside. The agent gathers the task context itself and proposes changes one at a time, each with a diff and a confirmation. Over MCP, the same tools work in Cursor, Claude Code and Codex.

HopkeymacOS 13+ · v1.7 · free · open source

Double-tap ⌘. The small stuff, at hand.

A palette in the menu bar: double-tap the right ⌘ and press one letter. An issue number from chat opens as a link, whether it’s Jira, GitHub, GitLab or your own pattern. A meeting link or a canned reply goes in with a digit, and the mic mutes system-wide before a call. Everything stays local, with no account, and it works in any keyboard layout.

SeatkeymacOS · Apple Silicon · OpenAI / Anthropic API

Your Claude Code subscription instead of an API key.

Obsidian, Raycast, Open WebUI and your own scripts ask for an OpenAI API key. Seatkey serves a compatible endpoint on 127.0.0.1 and answers through Claude Code, Codex or Cursor — on the subscription you already have. No separate key, no second bill.

CopyHomemacOS 13+ · SSH · MySQL and folders

Servers fail. Your copy is already home.

Backups of websites and MySQL databases to your Mac. On a schedule, CopyHome connects over SSH, dumps databases and archives folders, downloads and verifies every file, and can put a copy in Yandex Disk. Nothing to install on the server.

SashAndroid 10+ · v1.3 · free · open source

The Mac is in the other room. Its windows are in your hand.

Your Mac’s windows and its whole desktop on an Android phone or tablet. A tap is a click, the lower half of the screen is a trackpad, ⌘ and shift latch for shortcuts, and the clipboard works both ways. Over Wi‑Fi at home, through Tailscale when you’re out. The Mac runs the free Sill app, unchanged; Sash is an unofficial client for it.
